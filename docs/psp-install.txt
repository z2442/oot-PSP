OOT PSP installation

1. Extract this archive and copy the OOT_PSP folder to PSP/GAME on your
   PSP memory stick. Keep the Plugins and data folders beside EBOOT.PBP.

2. Put your own big-endian, compressed NTSC 1.0 ROM in
   PSP/GAME/OOT_PSP/data/basrom.z64. The ROM must be 32 MiB with CRC32 CD16C529.
   The filename baserom.z64 is also accepted.

3. Launch OOT PSP Port from the PSP Game menu. On first launch, wait for ROM
   validation and asset conversion to finish. Later launches reuse the generated
   data/segments/oot_psp_assets.bin file.

Installed layout:

PSP/GAME/OOT_PSP/
    EBOOT.PBP
    README.txt
    Plugins/dvemgr.prx
    data/basrom.z64       (add your own ROM)

Plugins/dvemgr.prx enables TV output on supported PSP models. Open HOME >
Video Settings to configure it. A PSP-1000 uses its LCD.

To regenerate assets, delete data/segments/oot_psp_assets.bin and launch again
with the ROM still present.

The archive includes the compiled port and its DVE module. You supply the ROM;
no ROM or converted game assets are included in this download.

HOME > Video Settings > Frame Rate selects 20 FPS (default) or 60 FPS.
Select Save video.ini to keep the setting. Game logic retains its original
update rate; 60 FPS adds interpolated presentations. LCD/240p use triple
buffering; 480i/480p keep double buffering to fit VRAM. Actual FPS depends on
the scene and hardware. See docs/psp-frame-presentation.md in the source tree.
